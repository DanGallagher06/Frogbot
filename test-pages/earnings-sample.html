Lilypad Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

POND CITY, Oregon — Lilypad Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: LILY), a maker of wetland-friendly outdoor furniture, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Revenue rose to $482.6 million, up 14.2% year over year, driven by strong demand for the company's Marsh Series loungers.1

Net income for Q3 2026 was $61.3 million, compared with $47.9 million in Q3 2025. Diluted earnings per share were $1.27, ahead of the $1.12 reported a year earlier. Gross margin expanded 180 basis points to 43.5%, reflecting lower resin costs and a more favourable product mix.

"This was our strongest quarter since going public," said Chief Executive Officer Rana Greenleaf. "We hopped past our own targets and we're carrying that momentum into the holiday season."

Third Quarter Highlights

  • Revenue of $482.6 million, up 14.2% from $422.6 million in the prior-year quarter.
  • Operating income of $84.0 million, an operating margin of 17.4%.
  • Direct-to-consumer sales grew 22% and now represent 38% of total revenue.
  • Free cash flow of $55.8 million; cash and equivalents of $310.2 million at quarter end.
  • Repurchased 1.1 million shares for $48.5 million.

Segment Performance

The Outdoor Living segment generated revenue of $331.4 million, up 11.8%, while the newer Garden Water segment grew 19.7% to $151.2 million. Garden Water's operating margin improved to 12.6% from 9.9% a year ago as the company's second fountain factory reached full production in July.

International revenue increased 8.3% to $96.5 million. Excluding currency effects, international growth would have been 10.1%.

Fourth Quarter and Full-Year 2026 Outlook

For the fourth quarter of 2026, Lilypad expects revenue between $505 million and $525 million and diluted EPS between $1.30 and $1.38. The company is raising its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $1.86 billion to $1.88 billion, up from its previous range of $1.80 billion to $1.84 billion. Full-year gross margin is now expected to be around 43%, about 50 basis points higher than earlier guidance.

Capital expenditures for fiscal 2026 are expected to be approximately $70 million, primarily for the expansion of the Pond City distribution centre.

About Lilypad Holdings

Founded in 1998, Lilypad Holdings designs and manufactures outdoor furniture and garden water features sold through more than 2,400 retail partners and its own online store. The company is headquartered at 742 Bullrush Avenue, Pond City, Oregon.
