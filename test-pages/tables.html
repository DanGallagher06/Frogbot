Pondweed Group PLC interim results for H1 2026/27

Pondweed Group PLC today reported sales of £2,410m for H1 2026/27, versus £2,265m in the prior year. Operating margin rose 0.4 ppts to 7.6%.

Shareholders received £120m ($152m) in dividends during the half.

We are raising our full-year capital expenditure guidance to c.£1.1bn (from c.£1.0bn).
